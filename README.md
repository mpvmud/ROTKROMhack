# Romance of the Three Kingdoms: Expanded

A major hack of Romance of the Three Kingdoms for the NES, by Kilith.

Make your own ruler, build custom officers, draft your generals like a football team, or start back in 184 with the Yellow Turbans.

## Download

Grab the latest version from the **[Releases](https://github.com/mpvmud/ROTKROMhack/releases)** page.

This is a patch only. You need your own copy of **Romance of the Three Kingdoms (USA)** for the NES. No ROMs are provided here, so please don't ask for one.

## How to patch

1. Download the latest release zip.
2. Use the BPS patch with an online patcher or Floating IPS (Flips).
3. Pick your clean ROM and the patch, save the result, and play.

The BPS patch checks your ROM first and tells you if it's the wrong version. The full readme in the zip has the checksums, supported emulators and known issues.

## What's in it

* Make your own ruler and up to 10 custom officers
* 94 new historical officers, for a roster of 360
* New scenarios: 184, The Yellow Turban Rebellion, and The Draft
* Save and load with 5 save slots
* Much faster menus and computer turns
* An Options menu for disasters, stat pools, message speed and more
* Books and Power training to build up your officers
